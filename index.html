# jot

a scratchpad for your mac that does the math in the margin.

press ⌥A anywhere to open it. press it again to put it away.

↓ download for macabout 1 MB

or brew install lsuryatej/jot/jot

macOS 14 or later · free · open source

first time opening it? jot isn't notarized (there's no paid apple developer account behind it), so macOS asks first. open it once, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. brew skips this step.

## what's in it

  • - math that works. variables, percentages, units, currency6000
  • - checklists. type list on the first line and every line becomes one
  • - 5m timer or pomodoro 25/5, with confetti when it's done4:59
  • - remind tomorrow 9am sends a real macOS notification9:00 am
  • - paste images. ⇧⌘V reads the text out of a screenshotoffline
  • - **bold**, ==highlights==, headings, and long links that shrink to their domain
  • - ⇧⌘F searches every note at once
  • - sync across devices. not yet, honestlysomeday

it's all plain text on disk. a note still reads fine in cat, Obsidian or GitHub.

one anonymous update check a day, which you can turn off.

live currency rates stay off until you turn them on.
